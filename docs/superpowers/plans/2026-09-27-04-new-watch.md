# The New Watch, Live — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The living-particles design becomes the real dashboard at `/`, with the voice, power, stats and shortcut pages acting on the PC; the first dashboard moves to `/classic`.

**Architecture:** The approved prototype (`prototypes/living-particles.html`, in the repo) is split once into `watch_pc_controller/watch.html` (markup and CSS, without the demo side panel) and `watch_pc_controller/static/watch/app.js` (the particle engine and pages). Every change after that is an exact edit to those two files, so the design stays the one that was approved. A small `pc()` layer carries the pairing token and reports when the PC is away; each page swaps its demo data for the real endpoints. The Claude page and the games page stay hidden (plans 5 and 6), so nothing on screen is a demo.

**Tech Stack:** vanilla JS in the page (no build step), the existing FastAPI endpoints (`/api/status`, `/api/volume`, `/api/command`, `/api/power`, `/api/stats`, `/api/actions*`), `static/voice.js` from plan 3, Node 22 + Chrome over CDP for tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-claude-voice-design.md` — section 5 (the design language), section 10 step 6 ("חיבור לדשבורד האמיתי, ביחד עם העברת העיצוב החדש של כל הדפים"), section 7 row 1 ("המחשב לא זמין"). ## Global Constraints

- The design is the approved prototype: engine, formations, timings, colours and copy stay as they are; only data sources, the demo panel and demo copy change. No fireworks, no lines (the rules agreed on the prototype).
- Every request goes through `pc()`: `Authorization: Bearer <localStorage.pcToken>` when present; a 401 calls `window.pcGoPair()` (→ `/pair`).
- The status bar shows `מחובר`, or `המחשב לא זמין` when a status poll fails (spec 7).
- Polls: status (volume + reachability) every 3 s, stats every 2 s, open apps every 2.5 s.
- Volume from the slider or crown: at most one `POST /api/volume` per 120 ms; a volume set by hand is not overwritten by a poll for 1.5 s.
- Power: the request is sent when the hold completes (lock and sleep: on the tap); the watch's toast after the animation says whether the PC accepted it. Hold times come from `GET /api/power`.
- Shortcuts: the first six of the PC's `actions.json`, in its order; the page waits at most 4 s for them.
- Hidden until their plans: Claude (plan 5) and games (plan 6); a hidden page has no dot and cannot be reached by swipe or `goTo`. Game covers are copyrighted and never committed; the hidden games page draws monograms.
- The watch fits a phone: scale from the layout width (`clientWidth`), 306 px for the watch with its crown, 10 px each side; nothing spills sideways.
- Tests never act on the real PC: the live-page tests install a fake `fetch` in the page and record requests.

## Review Focus

1. **The PC goes away while the watch is open** (sleep, Wi‑Fi drops) → the status bar says `המחשב לא זמין` and recovers by itself when the PC answers again. Pinned in Task 2 (`when the PC stops answering the status bar says so, and recovers`).
2. **A drag on the volume slider** sends dozens of moves → one request per 120 ms carrying the latest value, and the next poll does not snap the slider back. Pinned in Task 2 (`moving the slider moves the PC, one request for a burst of moves`, `a volume just set is not overwritten by the next poll`).
3. **Letting go of restart or shutdown before the hold completes** → nothing is sent. Pinned in Task 4 (`letting go of a hold early sends nothing`).
4. **The PC refuses a power action or a shortcut** → an error toast, not a false "done". Pinned in Task 4 (`a refusal from the PC is shown as an error`) and Task 6 (`a failed action says so`).
5. **The prototype's demo leaks into the real watch** (the fake "Robox is waiting" in the status bar, the Claude demo, game covers) → never. Pinned in Task 1 (`the demo's Claude project never shows in the status bar`, `only the live pages have a dot`, `test_nothing_of_the_prototype_demo_is_left_on_the_page`).
